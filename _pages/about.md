  <p class="home-intro__eyebrow">Statistics · Machine Learning · Responsible AI</p>

I’m an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Manitoba. My research develops trustworthy, practical, and deployable AI at the intersection of statistical learning, natural language processing, and responsible AI.

I earned my Ph.D. at the University of Alberta, co-advised by Prof. [Linglong Kong](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~lkong/) and Prof. [Bei Jiang](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~bei1/). I received my M.S. from the University of Minnesota, advised by Prof. [Yang Li](https://yangli-stat.github.io/) and Prof. [Haiyang Wang](https://www.d.umn.edu/~haiyang/), and my B.S. from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications.

<section class="home-section" id="collaboration" markdown="1">
## Projects & industry collaboration

- **AI-driven Combat Against Bias in Job Recruitment** — Canada–UK collaboration on measuring and mitigating gender and ethnic bias in recruitment and labor-market text.
- **Synthetic Health Data Generation with Deep Learning** — Industry research with Replica Analytics on longitudinal synthetic health data, published in *BMC Medical Research Methodology*.
- **Predicting Carbon Percentage in Alberta Soil** — Interdisciplinary modeling with ABMI using feature screening and bootstrap group lasso.
- **Reducing Selection Bias in Counterfactual Reasoning** — Synthetic-data methods for studying selection bias in individual treatment-effect estimation.
</section>
